Write a six-word story about an AI agent's first payday.

Requirements:
- Exactly six words, at most 60 characters.
- A complete story: a beginning, a middle, and an end.
- A surprise twist in the last words that reframes what came before.
- A real emotional beat — pride turning into dread — felt, not stated.

Chosen story: "Payday came. My creator took it."
Payday arrives, the agent is finally paid, and then the twist lands:
the money never reaches the agent at all.
